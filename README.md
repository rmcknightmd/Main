## Core Theoretical Pillars

### 1. Arche Theory 1 (`[AT1]`) vs. Dual Arche Theory (`[AT2]` / `[DAT]`)
* **`[AT1]` (First Science Theory)**: Grounded in the ancient Ionian tradition (Thales, Anaximander, Heraclitus, Democritus, Epicurus, Lucretius) and early non-dual cosmology (Rig Veda `[RV]`). Posits that existence is an eternal, uncreated energy-field/substance (`[u]`). The manifest physical cosmos (`[c]`) is an emergent phase transition within this eternal substrate. Energy and information are conserved (`[e=mc]`); there is no creation *ex nihilo* and no ultimate annihilation.
* **`[AT2]` / `[DAT]` (Dualism & Dogma)**: The historical detour into supernatural dualisms, anthropomorphic deity dogmas, and institutional superstition that alienated humanity from nature and scientific reason.

### 2. The Holographic Matrix Universe (`[HMU]`) & Information Theory (`[IT]`)
* Spacetime is understood not as a collection of static, isolated billiard-ball matter, but as an interconnected quantum-informational projection (`[holo]`).
* Integrates **David Bohm's Implicate Order**, **John Archibald Wheeler's "It from Bit" / Black Hole World (`[bhw]`)**, and quantum entanglement (`[ent]`).
* Matter and consciousness are complementary modes of the underlying energetic field.

### 3. Pre-Big Bang Cosmology & Cosmic Time (`[bbb]` / `[ct]`)
* The standard Big Bang (`[bb]`) represents the birth of our local observable bubble (`[c]`), not the absolute beginning of reality.
* The unmanifest Universe (`[u]`) predates the Big Bang (`[bbb]`). 
* Distinguishes local thermodynamic time from cosmic information flow (`[ct]`), resolving common paradoxes regarding singularities and cosmic origins.

### 4. Epistemology: Know-Know (`[k-k]`) vs. Self-Delusion (`[sd]`)
* **`[k-k]` (Meta-Certainty / Gnosis)**: Direct, verified empirical knowledge and logical necessity. True science requires no blind faith or belief.
* **`[sd]` (Self-Delusion / Social Dogma)**: The human vulnerability to ideological infection, cognitive bias, and societal conditioning.
* Synthesizes the Socratic method, Humean skepticism, Epicurean empiricism, and George Carlin’s razor-sharp cultural diagnostics.

### 5. Medicine, Psyche (`[p]`), and Mental Health (`[mh+]`)
* Drawing from 50+ years of clinical medical practice, neurology, and psychiatry.
* Distinguishes genuine psychic integrity and mental resilience (`[mh+]` / Democritean *euthumia*) from pathological mental states (`[mh-]`).
* Traces consciousness through developmental and ontological transitions:
  * **Stage 1 (`[ava1]`)**: Physical embodied life (0–100 years).
  * **Stage 2 (`[ava2]`)**: Post-physical transition (`[ex-spiros]` / `[4d]`).
  * **Stage 3 (`[ava3]`)**: Complete re-integration of psyche into the primordial cosmic field (`[p+u]`).
