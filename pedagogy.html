Pedagogical & Grade-Level Curriculum Framework

Dr. Ray's manuscript organizes scientific concepts, critical thinking principles, and metaphysical models into tiered pedagogical levels ([gr1] through [gr9+]).

Grade 1 Tier ([gr1]) Elementary Core

Foundational identity and intuitive models: "What are you? You are a psyche/person in the cosmos." Focuses on introducing energy, light, and the wonder of nature without dogmatic confusion.

Grade 6 Tier ([gr6]) Intermediate Logic

Mechanisms of physics, scientific method ([SM]), plate tectonics, cosmology, and identifying fallacies. Focuses on question formation and empirical verification.

Grade 9+ Tier ([gr9]) Advanced Synthesis

Grand Unified Theories (GUT), holographic matrices, historical philosophy of science (Pre-Socratics to Quantum Era), epistemological delusion analysis ([sd]), and mental health philosophy ([mh+]).
